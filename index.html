GOOD TO KNOW

Delivery & returns.

Delivery: Delivery fees, estimated dispatch times and courier options will be confirmed with you before payment. Please make sure your delivery address and contact number are correct.

HERE TO HELP

Frequently asked questions.

How do I place an order?

Add your chosen dress and size to your bag, then select Order via WhatsApp. We will confirm availability, delivery and the final total with you.

When should I pay?

Please wait for VELÉRA to confirm your order total and availability. We will then provide the correct payment instructions or payment link. Do not pay an unconfirmed amount.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timing depends on the product and courier. We will confirm the estimated delivery timeframe before you pay.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible on WhatsApp. Changes depend on whether the order has already been placed with the supplier or dispatched.

How do I choose the right size?

Use the size information on the product listing and our size guide. If you are unsure, message us before ordering.
